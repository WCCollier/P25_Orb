P25 Orb A proposed ARC Edge add-on — proof of concept RX-only, unlicensed tier

When a P25 trunk saturates, officers press the button and nothing goes out. The trunk knows. Nobody else does. P25 Orb listens to that trunk and tells the on-scene commander what never got through.

Presenter View

The RF Environment and the Command Feed, framed side by side in one tab, so the audience sees the Command Feed reacting the moment the traffic happens rather than taking that on trust. The RF Environment pane collapses to a thin rail when you want their full attention on the Command Feed alone — it keeps running underneath, just out of the way. The zoom control trades visible area for stroke weight, which is the right trade when the far end is watching compressed video.

Running the demo

  1. Open Presenter View. Both panes load and start talking to each other automatically.
  2. Click into the RF Environment pane and press Play. It drives the Command Feed pane; the Command Feed has no timer of its own.
  3. Space plays and pauses, R resets — both with focus in the RF Environment pane. The numbered buttons jump to any of the seven beats for rehearsal.
  4. The whole sequence runs 2 minutes 52 seconds at 1×. There is a 2× option for rehearsal.
What is real and what is simulated. The detection, synthesis and alarm engine is real working code, and the AI classifications were genuinely produced by Claude Haiku 4.5 and cached so the presentation needs no network. The radio events themselves are a scripted timeline, because no P25 receiver hardware exists for this demo. ARC Edge's own path-selection is represented narratively, since that is Orb Aerospace's own intellectual property and not ours to implement.
